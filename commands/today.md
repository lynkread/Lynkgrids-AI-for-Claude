Use the `lynkgrids` skill. Read-only. Scope: $ARGUMENTS

1. Plan: status, and the end date if it's a trial or ending soon.
2. Queued today (counts by campaign).
3. Replies to handle (name, one-line gist).
4. Waiting on you (follow-ups and overdue tasks).
5. Campaigns: sent, accepted, replied.
6. The three things to do first.

Do not send, launch, import, or change anything.
